# Governance of Retraceur

The **Retraceur** open source project is managed in a transparent, collaborative, and inclusive manner.  
This document defines the governance structure, roles and responsibilities, and decision-making processes.  

---

## 1. Principles
- **Openness**: anyone can propose ideas, report bugs, or contribute.  
- **Transparency**: all important discussions are public (issues, pull requests, GitHub discussions).  
- **Benevolent meritocracy**: responsibilities are earned through engagement and community trust.  
- **Integrity**: decisions are made in the community’s best interest, in line with the [Conflict of Interest Policy](./CONFLICT_OF_INTEREST.md).  

---

## 2. Roles and Responsibilities

### Contributors
- Anyone who submits code, documentation, tests, or ideas via issues/pull requests.  
- Rights: participate in discussions, submit contributions, propose improvements.  
- Responsibilities: follow the [Code of Conduct](./CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md) and the [Contribution Guide](./CONTRIBUTING.md).  

### Maintainers
- Responsible for reviewing and merging contributions.  
- Rights: merge PRs, manage releases, organize the backlog.  
- Responsibilities: ensure quality, guide contributors, enforce governance rules.  

### Governance Committee
- A group of individuals designated to make strategic decisions.  
- Rights: define the roadmap, resolve disputes, manage conflicts of interest.  
- Responsibilities: represent the community, ensure fairness, safeguard the project’s sustainability.  

---

## 3. Decision-Making

### Day-to-day decisions (issues, PRs)
- Made by maintainers, with an effort to reach consensus.  
- In case of disagreement, discussions continue until a compromise is found.  

### Strategic decisions (governance, roadmap)
- Discussed publicly (issues or discussions).  
- If consensus cannot be reached, a governance committee vote is held.  
- Each committee member has one vote.  
- In case of a tie, the project lead’s vote counts double.  

---

## 4. Transparency
- All decisions, votes, and resolutions are documented publicly (issues or wiki).  
- Conflict of interest declarations are managed according to the [dedicated policy](./CONFLICT_OF_INTEREST.md).  

---

## 5. Revision
This document may be amended by the community via pull request.  
Changes must be approved by consensus or by a governance committee vote.

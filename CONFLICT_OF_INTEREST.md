# Conflict of Interest Policy

This policy aims to ensure transparency, fairness, and trust within the Retraceur community.  

---

## 1. When to declare a conflict of interest?
A conflict of interest arises when a personal, professional, or financial situation may influence — or appear to influence — your contributions or decisions.  

Examples:  
- working for a company offering a competing service,  
- promoting a solution for commercial reasons,  
- participating in decisions related to an organization you are affiliated with.  

---

## 2. How to declare?

- Create an *issue* with the `conflict-of-interest` label.  
- Briefly describe the situation. 

---

## 3. Example of a public declaration
```markdown
### Conflict of interest – [Your name or GitHub handle]  
- Nature of the conflict: [e.g. employment at a competing company]  
- Potential impact: [e.g. I will abstain from voting on related decisions]  
- Date: [YYYY-MM-DD]  
```
---

## 4. What happens next?

The governance committee will review the declaration. Depending on the case, you may:

- continue contributing normally,
- abstain from voting on certain topics,
- withdraw from a specific discussion.

Decisions will be communicated transparently to the community, unless sensitive information requires confidentiality.

---

## 5. Respect and confidentiality

All declarations are handled seriously and respectfully. Sensitive information will only be shared if necessary to protect the integrity of the project.

---

## 6. Revision

This policy may be revised periodically to remain aligned with the project’s evolution and community needs.
